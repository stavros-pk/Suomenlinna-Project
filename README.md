### Propulsion and Azipod Desing 
Desingning the propulsion of the system was quite challenging due to the complexity of the configuartion, the availiability of tools and the small scale of the whole configuration.  
The first problem that required a solution was the intallation of the system in such a way to not let any leaks inside the hull. Next step in the design process was the indepentent movement of the propeller as well as the orientation of the POD itself. The third step was to connect the Motor of the model to the propeller. The difficulty of the above steps was the size, and the physical restrictions of the model's geometry.
## Integrated Components
